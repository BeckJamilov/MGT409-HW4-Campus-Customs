PASSED

2. Dynamic search result cards after a category question

PASSED

After typing "what hoodies do you have?" on the Home page, the site switched to the Products page and showed 27 product cards under a "From your chat" banner, next to the agent's text answer. The 27 cards are exactly the 27 hood-type products in the database. They come from the structured products list in the API response, not from parsing the reply text.

PASSED

On the Products page, choosing Category = Hoodies, Size in stock = XL, and Sort by = Price: low to high narrowed 102 items to 21, sorted from $45.00 up to $88.00. The 21 products are exactly those the database lists as hoodies with XL quantity above 0.
